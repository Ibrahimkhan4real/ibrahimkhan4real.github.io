## What connects them?

The energy projects are about making better choices when we do not know exactly
what will happen next. The Tic-Tac-Toe project shows the same planning idea in a
much smaller and more visual problem.

The meeting transcriber is a bit different. I made it because long supervision
meetings can contain a lot of useful details, and it is easy to forget a small
action point afterwards. The tool keeps the audio private during transcription,
then turns it into notes which are easier to use.

I will keep adding to these repos when I have something useful to share. You can
also see the rest of them on [my GitHub](https://github.com/Ibrahimkhan4real?tab=repositories).
